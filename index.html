About the Chef

Fifteen courses, one evening, and an hour where the outside world quiets down.

I trained in Modena under Giulia Rava, ran the pasta station at Osteria del Mare in Rome, and opened a forty-seat tasting counter in Brooklyn before going private in 2019. Now I cook for your table, your living room, your rooftop — with seasonal Italian technique and a deep respect for whoever I'm feeding.

My cooking is restrained. I buy less and taste more. I'd rather serve four plates perfectly than twelve that merely arrive. Every menu is written for the night it'll be eaten — never reheated, never repeated exactly.
